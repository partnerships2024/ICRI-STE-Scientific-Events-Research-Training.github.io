ICRI-STE Evaluation Policy

Evaluator Nomination & Responsibilities

The evaluator framework supports independent, objective and academically rigorous assessment of research submissions across the ICRI-STE Global Research Community.

Under the executive direction of the President or Vice Presidents of ICRI-STE, and in consultation with the respective Lab Directors, evaluators shall be nominated to assess Research Concept Notes and Comprehensive Research Proposals.

Evaluators may be selected from senior members of the respective ICRI-STE Labs, members of the concerned Lab Commissions, or qualified academic experts, including Full Professors and other senior academics from recognized national and international universities affiliated with, or relevant to, the respective Lab’s research domain.

The evaluation process shall cover research submissions from the broader ICRI-STE Global Research Community, including Principal Scientists, Senior Scientists, Junior Scientists, and Early Career Researchers (ECRs) at the ECR1-BS (Undergraduate), ECR2-MS (Master’s), and ECR3-PhD (Doctoral) levels.

All nominated evaluators are expected to provide independent, objective, and academically rigorous assessments in accordance with ICRI-STE’s research standards, ethical principles, and evaluation policies.
