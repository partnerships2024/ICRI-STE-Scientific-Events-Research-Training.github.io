Research Laboratories

ICRI-STE's principal laboratories connect computational healthcare, intelligent energy systems and environmental intelligence. Each laboratory operates within the Council's proposed institutional framework, subject to approved leadership, resources and governance.

Healthcare · Biomedical AI

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab

Computational healthcare, biomedical artificial intelligence, computational oncology, systems biology, medical data science and trustworthy intelligent healthcare. Research spans biomedical modelling, medical imaging, transcriptomics, federated learning and agentic research platforms.

Energy · Sustainability

Intelligent Energy Systems & Sustainable Technologies (IESST) Lab

Intelligent energy systems, renewable-energy forecasting and optimisation, smart grids, sustainable engineering and computational energy modelling for efficient and resilient energy technologies.

Environment · Autonomous systems

Environmental Intelligence & Autonomous Systems (EIAS) Lab
